# Instruments

- AGPM_questionnaire.md: consent text, instructions, rating scales and item structure of the four Jotform forms.
- AGPM_evidence_cards.csv: the evidence cards shown to raters (two points and sources per method and criterion, with source URLs).

Extracted from the live forms. Contains no responses, emails or participant data.
- AGPM_caps_classification.csv: author classification of the 80 governance cells (G2 to G5) as prescribed, optional or tailorable, with the basis and the card sources. Used by agpm_caps_sensitivity.py.
